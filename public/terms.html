Terms of use · Updated 23 October 2026

Crux is a personal project for planning and recording training. Anyone with a verified Google account can sign in to the live app without an invitation. Each account has its own planner.

Training recommendations

Suggestions use your logged activities and recovery check-ins. They are general training guidelines, not medical advice, diagnoses, professional coaching or guarantees against injury. Use your judgment, keep your records accurate, and seek qualified help when needed. You can always choose a different session or rest.

Your account and records

Use an account you control. Keep your sign-in secure, respect other users' privacy, and do not attempt to bypass the app's access rules. You retain ownership of the training content you enter. Crux processes it to provide the planner, offline saving and account synchronization described in the privacy policy.

Backups and availability

Crux is a small, evolving project without a guaranteed service level. Features or availability may change. Keep exported JSON backups, especially before importing or replacing records. Browser storage can be removed by device settings, and online saving depends on the connected services.

Support and access

Contact the project maintainer listed below for support or account deletion. Access to online features may be withdrawn; records and previously downloaded copies are handled as described in the privacy policy.
