Your training, your privacy.

Privacy policy · Updated 23 October 2026

Crux is a personal training planner. The live app requires Google sign-in before opening your planner. This page explains the information used by the app and its accounts.

Information you provide

Crux saves your training profiles, workout library, calendar, completed sessions, bouldering grade completion counts, optional exercise loads in kilograms, projects, notes and recovery check-ins. Your personal catalogue is saved with your account and is not a public catalogue. Only enter information you want to keep in your planner. JSON exports contain these records.

Google sign-in

If you are signed out, Crux opens Google sign-in automatically. When you complete sign-in, Google and Supabase provide basic identity information: your account identifier, email address, name and profile picture. Supabase stores identity information to authenticate you. Crux uses your identity to open your own private planner. Anyone with a verified Google account can sign in without an invitation.

Crux requests only basic sign-in scopes. It does not request your Gmail messages, Drive files or contacts. Google user data is used for authentication and account access, not advertising, sale or AI model training.

Where information is stored

  • Earlier device-only planners remain in this browser's local storage. You can explicitly restore them into your account after signing in.
  • Signed-in planners are stored in Supabase and cached in the browser for offline use. Training records are sent to Supabase when synchronizing.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored by the Supabase client in the browser. They are excluded from training exports.
  • Your training records and personal catalogue are not shared with other Crux users.

Database rules keep one person's training separate from other app users. The project owner administers the backend and can access stored account data. Local browser storage is not encrypted storage.

Optional calendar connection

If you create a private calendar link, anyone who has that link can read the selected profile's planned workout dates, names, locations and exercise sets, reps, rest and optional planned loads in kilograms. It excludes recovery check-ins, training history, notes, projects and account details. Calendar apps you subscribe with, including Apple Calendar and iCloud if selected, receive this planned-workout information. No Apple account credentials or Google Calendar permissions are requested.

Subscription links use random access tokens; only their hashes are stored in the database. The app shows a link when you create it and excludes it from training backups. Disable or replace the link in Crux to stop future reads through that URL. Unsubscribe in your calendar app to remove its saved copy; revocation cannot erase copies already downloaded. Calendar files you export also contain the selected planned workouts and do not update automatically.

Services used

GitHub Pages hosts the website, Google provides Google sign-in, and Supabase provides online authentication and storage. These services may process connection and security metadata, such as IP addresses and login timestamps, under their own policies. Crux does not add advertising trackers or third-party analytics.

Your choices and deletion

You can export your planner or sign out. A fully synchronized account copy is removed from this browser on sign-out; unsynchronized edits are retained separately for your next sign-in. Browser settings can remove local data. Keep a JSON backup before clearing it.

Online records remain until removed by the project owner. For account or data deletion, contact the project maintainer listed below. Removing Google access or signing out does not automatically delete online records or copies you have downloaded.

Questions

Contact the project maintainer for account, privacy or deletion requests. The public project and maintainer are listed on GitHub. Do not post private training records or credentials in public issues.
